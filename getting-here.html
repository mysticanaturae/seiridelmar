Your arrival, made easier

From runway to shoreline.

Hopkins is in Belize’s Stann Creek District, on the Caribbean coast.

Most guests arrive through Philip S. W. Goldson International Airport (BZE), then continue by domestic flight, shuttle or rental car. Published journey times are estimates; confirm the details and transfers for your flight directly with the resort and provider.

Seiri del Mar lists a flight-and-shuttle option, a direct shuttle and a rental-car route. Ask the concierge to help arrange transport: info@belizehopkins.com.

Three ways to arrive

Pick your route.

Official Seiri del Mar estimates from Belize City are approximate. Allow extra time for check-in, connections, weather and road conditions.

01
By air / then shuttle

Fly to Dangriga.

Take a domestic flight from Belize City to Dangriga, then continue by pre-arranged shuttle to Seiri del Mar. A flight-and-shuttle connection is often around 45 minutes in total; the final road transfer from Dangriga is roughly 20 minutes. Confirm schedules and pickup details when booking.

Book the onward transfer ahead of time and confirm the flight schedule with Tropic Air.

02
Direct transfer

Ride with a shuttle.

A private or shared transfer takes you from Belize City to Hopkins without a rental car. Allow around 1 hour 45 minutes for the drive; actual timing depends on pickup point, stops and road conditions.

Contact the resort to arrange a provider and confirm current pricing.

03
At your own pace

Rent a car.

Drive south from Belize City and follow the route recommended by your rental provider and the resort. Allow around 1 hour 45 minutes on the route recommended by your rental provider; check directions before setting off.

Drive in daylight where possible and allow flexibility for traffic, rain and stops.

04
Alternative / scenic drive

Consider the Coastal Road.

The Coastal Road (also called Coastal Plains Highway) can offer a shorter overland connection toward southern Belize. It is a useful alternative when conditions are good, though it is not always the quickest route to Hopkins.

Check that it is open and ask your rental company or Seiri del Mar about conditions before choosing it. Heavy rain can affect access and travel time.
