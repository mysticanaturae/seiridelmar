The vision behind the name

From a question to a life that feels more your own.

Every meaningful journey begins somewhere: with a question, a longing, a moment of recognition or the quiet feeling that a new chapter is ready to begin.

MY BLINKITA was created as a meeting place for different paths of exploration and personal development. Rather than placing every experience inside one method, it makes room for the distinct practices, knowledge and traditions that have shaped the work of Blanka Thomas.

Here, personal insight can meet the wisdom of the traditional Maya calendar. Body-based practices can sit alongside hypnotherapy and guided reflection. Online sessions can open a door from anywhere in the world, while Belize offers a setting for selected in-person experiences, retreats and time by the Caribbean Sea.

The intention is not to tell you who to become. It is to offer thoughtful ways to listen, explore, reconnect and make choices with greater awareness.

The person behind BLINKITA

Meet Blanka Thomas.

Known as Maginja Blinkita, Blanka is a practitioner, guide and lifelong student whose work has grown through years of personal experience, study, initiation and practice.

Her path brings together traditional Maya time wisdom, Munay Ki, hypnotherapy, coaching, bodywork and energy-based practices. These are distinct approaches, each with its own roots and context. BLINKITA creates a space where people can explore the approach that speaks to their present needs.

A path shaped over time

Study, practice and lived experience.

2003

The first encounter with Tzolk'in

Blanka's exploration of the traditional Maya calendar began a long relationship with its cycles, day signs and understanding of time.

2008

Munay Ki & the path of practice

Initiated into Munay Ki, she continued exploring energy work, personal development and the relationship between inner change and everyday life.

2011

Initiation in Guatemala

After years of study, Blanka was initiated by Tata Pedro Cruz in Guatemala in the Saq' I'x tradition, deepening her commitment to the Maya calendar and its living context.

Her work continues to evolve through practice, learning and the people she meets. The story is not presented as a finished destination, but as an ongoing relationship with knowledge, experience and service.

What guides the work

Room for depth. Respect for each path.

Respect for roots

Traditional knowledge is not treated as a trend or blended into a single universal system. Its origins, cultural context and distinct practices deserve care.

Your journey remains yours

There is no single route everyone must follow. You can begin with one conversation, continue with a programme or simply take the insight you came for.

Depth without pressure

Transformation is personal. BLINKITA offers experiences and guidance, while leaving each person free to decide what is useful and what comes next.

Online and in the world

You can connect from anywhere. Belize is an invitation for those who want to experience selected work in person, never a requirement.
