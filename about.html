Thirty beachside villas, open sea air and the unhurried spirit of Hopkins.

Set along the Caribbean shoreline in Hopkins, Seiri del Mar is a boutique resort designed around privacy, comfort and connection to place. Landscaped gardens meet the sea, and the village’s welcoming Garifuna culture is part of the wider experience.

Choose a one, two or three-bedroom villa, find a quiet moment by the water, or let the resort team help shape your days in Belize. Villa layouts and services vary, so confirm the details that matter to your stay directly with Seiri del Mar.

Belize, beyond the shoreline

Rooted in the rhythm of Hopkins.

Hopkins is known for its vibrant Garifuna culture, shaped by music, drumming, dance, food and oral traditions passed between generations. From the village to the reef and rainforest, Belize invites you to explore with curiosity and respect.

Seiri del Mar makes a welcoming base for discovering the coast. Ask the resort team about locally guided experiences, practical arrangements and what is available during your dates.
