A Sunrise Ritual is an invitation to begin slowly: with gentle movement, breath, cacao and a little space to arrive in the day and in the place around you.

Enquire about Sunrise
A ritual at first light

Wake the body.
Whisper the wish.

Before the sun clears the horizon, the morning asks us to pause. We arrive with breath, gentle movement and a moment of gratitude for the place we have come to.

This is not a class to keep up with. It is a small ceremony of renewal, shaped around the people present and the quiet beauty of the Caribbean dawn.

We begin by listening—to the tide, to the first birds, to the body waking. Then the ritual may unfold through the Five Tibetan Rites, a flowing Sun Salutation, cacao and a personal intention for the day.

01

Cross the threshold

Meet the early light in stillness. Breathe, feel the ground beneath you and let the night give way at its own pace.

02

Awaken through the Five Rites

When suitable, we explore the Five Tibetan Rites as a gentle, mindful movement practice. They are sometimes wrapped in the modern “Fountain of Youth” story; here, that name is held as a poetic invitation to renewal, not a promise to reverse ageing or treat illness.

03

Offer a greeting to the sun

Surya Namaskar, or Sun Salutation, is traditionally taught as a sequence of 12 postures. We move with the breath, choosing a comfortable number of rounds for the people and the morning—never chasing a number for its own sake.

04

Whisper your intention

As the sun rises, bring to mind a wish, a quality or a new beginning you want to welcome. You may whisper it toward the light, hold it quietly in your heart, or write it down to carry with you.

05

Let cacao seal the moment

Share cacao and a little stillness. Leave with one word, image or feeling to guide the day ahead—and the freedom to let the rest unfold.

Every dawn is a doorway. This is your invitation to step through it awake.
