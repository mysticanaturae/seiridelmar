The Sunset Ritual is a personal introduction to the traditional 260-day Maya calendar, cacao and an experience shaped by time, place and the individual.

Rather than a fixed performance, it can be a thoughtful gathering: a warm cup, a story about cyclical time, an invitation to reflect and space for the day to settle. The emphasis is on curiosity, presence and respectful listening.

The experience can be arranged privately or for a small group. The format, ingredients, timing, facilitator and availability are confirmed directly with Seiri del Mar.

A moment in the cycle

Time is something we can meet.

The Maya Tzolk’in is a 260-day calendar count formed by the interweaving of thirteen numbers and twenty named days. Each date brings a unique pairing before the sequence begins again.

In this ritual, guests are invited to encounter the calendar through a personal reflection. The reading is offered as a point of curiosity and conversation—not a fixed prediction, a substitute for personal choice, or a claim that every Maya community interprets time in the same way.

Maya calendar traditions are living and diverse. Names, pronunciations and interpretations can vary across languages and communities; the experience will identify the tradition and approach being shared.

The Tzolk’in is not simply a personality quiz or a generic “Mayan horoscope.” It is part of a broader Maya understanding of time and ritual, and its practice continues in distinct communities today. This experience offers an introduction and a reflective conversation, with care for the source and limits of what is being shared.

Guests who wish to explore the calendar in advance can use the Living Time widget on the Seiri del Mar home page to plan a date and save a reflection in My Seiri.
