A private villa, a quiet shoreline and the Caribbean just beyond your veranda.

Seiri del Mar's beachfront resort in Hopkins brings together 30 villas across one, two and three-bedroom stays. The villas are set between 5 and 75 feet from the Caribbean Sea, with landscaped gardens, pools and a 212-foot dock close at hand.

Choose a compact coastal retreat, extra room for family and friends, or a spacious penthouse with room to spread out. Villa layouts and features vary, so confirm the details that matter to your stay directly with the resort.

Before you arrive

A few useful details.

Villa amenities vary by layout. The resort can confirm the exact features and guest capacity for the dates you have in mind.

Can we cook in the villa?

Official accommodation information says villas have fully equipped kitchens or kitchenettes. Confirm the setup for your selected villa.

Is housekeeping included?

The accommodation page lists daily housekeeping. Contact the resort to confirm the service schedule for your stay.

How close is the beach?

Villas are listed between 5 and 75 feet from the Caribbean Sea. Exact position depends on the villa.

How many guests can stay?

One-bedroom villas are listed for two guests; two-bedroom layouts vary, with some loft configurations accommodating up to six. Confirm the limit for your chosen villa.
