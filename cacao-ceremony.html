A contemporary cacao story

Meet IxCacao.

In some contemporary cacao circles, IxCacao is spoken of as a feminine spirit of cacao—an invitation to generosity, warmth and connection. Maya communities and traditions are diverse, and the historical record does not establish one single IxCacao legend shared by all Maya peoples.

In the contemporary reflection we share at Seiri, IxCacao places a cacao seed in human hands as a reminder that care grows through patience: a tree is tended, a fruit is opened, a drink is prepared and, at last, a cup is shared. The story invites us to listen, give thanks and notice what we bring into the circle.

This is a modern ceremonial retelling, not a translation of a verified ancient Maya text or a claim that one story represents every Maya tradition. We approach the living cultures of Belize with humility and respect.

Cacao & wellbeing

What research can—and cannot—say.

Cacao naturally contains theobromine, a mild stimulant, and cocoa flavanols. Research on some flavanol-rich cocoa products has found small average short-term changes in blood pressure. Those studies used specific products and doses; they do not show that a cacao ceremony treats or prevents illness, and their findings cannot be assumed to apply to every ceremonial drink.

Our gathering is a cultural, sensory and reflective experience—not medical care or a substitute for advice from your clinician.

If you are sensitive to stimulants, have a food allergy, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a health concern or medication question, please consult a qualified clinician and tell us before the gathering. Cacao is optional; we can discuss a no-cacao way to take part.

Arrive comfortably

We will confirm the ingredients and format with you in advance. Please let us know about allergies, sensitivities, accessibility needs or anything that would help you feel at ease. You are always welcome to participate in the reflection without drinking cacao.
