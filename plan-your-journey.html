What is the weather like when you come?

Choose a month to explore typical daytime highs, nighttime lows, average rainfall and the usual number of rain days around the Hopkins and Dangriga area. This makes it easier to choose when to visit, what to pack and what kind of journey you would like to plan.

Belize is warm throughout the year, but rainfall changes considerably by season. The drier months are generally February to April; wetter conditions become more common from June through November.

These are long-term climate averages, not a forecast for your travel dates. Local conditions can vary, and the wider Belize itinerary may include regions with different temperatures and rainfall.
Climate reference: Belize National Meteorological Service, 1991–2020 normals, Melinda Forest Station, approximately 9 miles from Dangriga. Rain days count days with more than 1 mm of rainfall. View official climate data ↗
