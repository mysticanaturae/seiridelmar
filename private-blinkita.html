A more personal way to restore

Private, personal, considered.

Some guests arrive knowing exactly what they need. Others prefer to explore the possibilities first. Private sessions create room for both: skilled practitioners, attentive conversation and experiences adapted to the individual rather than a fixed timetable.

Depending on the experience and availability, the BLINKITA offering may include restorative bodywork, Maya Abdominal Massage, Jin Shin Jyutsu, Aqua Lemu Reiki, personal Tzolk'in readings and other practices. Each session is discussed individually; suitability, timing and availability are confirmed with Seiri del Mar.
