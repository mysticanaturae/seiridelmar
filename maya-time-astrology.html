A sacred count of 260 days

Time as a path of becoming.

The Tzolk'in weaves 13 numbers with 20 day signs to create a cycle of 260 unique days. Across Maya communities, this sacred calendar is connected with the Moon, the Sun, the cultivation of corn and human gestation. In a BLINKITA reading, it becomes a contemplative lens for noticing the qualities, transitions and questions alive in your story.

The calendar does not dictate who you are or what must happen. It offers a way to listen more closely: to the season you are in, the patterns you carry and the possibilities opening before you. Many people experience this as a map for the evolution of awareness—an invitation to meet time with curiosity rather than rush through it.

A personal story of the path: In 2011 in Guatemala, Maya shaman Tata Pedro Cruz dedicated Blanka as Saq I'x—the White Jaguar, Guardian of Time. This lineage and naming are part of the personal story from which BLINKITA's time work grows.
