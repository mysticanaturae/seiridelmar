Travel with BLINKITA

Follow the story of the Maya world.

These are guided, multi-day travel concepts connecting places, landscapes, cultural heritage and selected encounters across Belize and neighbouring Maya regions. They are distinct from BLINKITA wellness retreats: the focus here is exploration, discovery and the wider journey.

Each itinerary will be shaped around the route, season, group and practical travel arrangements. The places shown are proposed highlights, not a promise that every stop is included in every departure.

The BLINKITA perspective

Travel through place. Experience Living Time.

Each expedition can include an introduction to the traditional 260-day Maya Tzolk'in calendar and a gentle thread of reflection throughout the journey. The places we visit give the story its geography; the people, landscapes and moments along the way give it life.

This is not a fixed ritual schedule or a substitute for local cultural knowledge. The Living Time element is facilitated by BLINKITA and shaped respectfully around the route, the group and the communities we meet.

13 days · Belize

13 Days · Belize

A first, immersive introduction to Belize: Caribbean coast, rainforest, rivers, local communities and selected ancient Maya sites. The route is designed as a south-to-north journey, finishing within reach of the international departure point. A balanced journey with time to explore, enjoy nature and experience the region beyond the usual resort stay.

Possible highlights: Hopkins and the Caribbean coast · Cayo and Xunantunich · Lamanai or Altun Ha · rainforest and river experiences.

20 days · Belize & Guatemala

20 Days · Belize & Guatemala

A deeper exploration of the Maya world, combining Belize’s archaeological landscapes and living culture with a journey into Guatemala’s Petén region and the monumental city of Tikal. More time is set aside for the forest and archaeological landscape around Flores.

Possible highlights: Belize’s Cayo region · official border crossing · Flores · Tikal National Park · jungle and lake landscapes.

33 days · Belize, Guatemala & Mexico

33 Days · The Maya World

Our most extensive journey through the wider Maya region. Alongside Belize and Guatemala, the route continues north into Mexico’s Yucatán Peninsula, with time for Tulum and other carefully selected places.

Possible highlights: Belize’s southern, central and northern regions · Tikal and Petén · Chetumal and Bacalar · Tulum and the Caribbean coast of Quintana Roo.

A closer look · Sample routes

Every journey has its own rhythm.

Explore the outline of each expedition. These sample day-by-day routes show how the regions could connect, while leaving enough flexibility for the season, local experiences, travel conditions and the rhythm of the group.

13 days · Belize

The Caribbean & Ancient Maya Introduction

+
Days 1–2 · Arrival & Hopkins

Arrive in Belize, transfer to the Caribbean coast and settle into the rhythm of Hopkins. Welcome, orientation and time to rest after the journey.

Day 3 · Garifuna & Caribbean life

Discover the coastal landscape, local food, music and living culture through a locally guided experience. Keep time open for the sea.

Day 4 · Into the Cayo region

Travel inland through Belize’s changing landscapes to the San Ignacio area, your base for the western part of the journey.

Day 5 · Cahal Pech & San Ignacio

Begin with the archaeological site of Cahal Pech and explore the town and its surroundings at an unhurried pace.

Day 6 · Xunantunich

Visit the Maya city reached by the hand-cranked ferry across the Mopan River. Explore the ceremonial centre with a knowledgeable local guide.

Day 7 · Cave & river landscapes

Choose a guided cave experience or a gentler nature day, subject to access, fitness requirements and current conditions.

Day 8 · Caracol or Mountain Pine Ridge

A full-day option into the Chiquibul forest to visit Caracol, or a more relaxed nature-focused alternative in the Mountain Pine Ridge area.

Day 9 · A slower day in Cayo

Time for rest, local discoveries, a river experience or an optional additional site. The journey needs breathing space too.

Day 10 · North to Orange Walk

Travel north through Belize, with a stop en route depending on the departure plan and road conditions.

Day 11 · Lamanai by river

Journey by boat through the New River landscape to Lamanai, where rainforest, wildlife and the ancient city become part of one experience.

Day 12 · Belize City or the coast

Return toward Belize City or choose a coastal/island extension if the departure and transfer logistics allow.

Day 13 · Departure

Transfer to the agreed departure point. International flight timing may require an additional overnight stay.

20 days · Belize & Guatemala

Tikal & The Heart of the Maya World

+
Days 1–4 · Hopkins & the Caribbean

Arrive in Belize, settle on the coast and experience the Caribbean landscape, local culture and a gentle introduction to the journey.

Days 5–9 · Cayo & ancient cities

Explore the San Ignacio region, with selected visits to Cahal Pech, Xunantunich and a guided cave or rainforest experience.

Day 10 · Caracol or a nature day

Allow a full day for Caracol and the forest route, or choose an alternative depending on road conditions and group interests.

Days 11–12 · Northern Belize

Travel north for a river and archaeological experience at Lamanai, with an overnight base selected to support the border journey.

Day 13 · Belize to Petén, Guatemala

Cross at the official Belize–Guatemala border at Melchor de Mencos, subject to current entry requirements and border operating conditions. Continue to Flores or the Lake Petén Itzá area.

Day 14 · Flores & Lake Petén Itzá

Settle into the Guatemalan Petén region, explore Flores and prepare for the archaeological days ahead.

Days 15–16 · Tikal National Park

Spend a full day exploring Tikal with an authorised guide, with an optional second visit or special sunrise/sunset entry if available and arranged in advance.

Day 17 · Yaxhá or a slower Petén day

An optional visit to Yaxhá or a quieter day around the lake and local communities, depending on group pace and logistics.

Day 18 · Living Petén

Time for nature, local food, cultural encounters and reflection on the ancient and contemporary Maya world.

Day 19 · Flexible day

Keep a buffer for weather, road conditions, an additional archaeological visit or simply more time in Flores.

Day 20 · Departure or onward travel

Transfer to Mundo Maya International Airport or continue with a separately arranged onward route.

33 days · Belize, Guatemala & Mexico

The Wider Maya World Expedition

+
Days 1–4 · Southern Belize & Hopkins

Arrive and settle on the Caribbean coast. Discover Hopkins, the surrounding landscape and selected local experiences, with time to rest.

Days 5–8 · Inland Belize & Cayo

Move into the Cayo region for selected archaeological sites such as Cahal Pech and Xunantunich, plus a cave or forest experience.

Days 9–11 · Caracol & northern Belize

Allow a full day for Caracol or an alternative nature experience, then continue north for a river journey and Lamanai, subject to route design.

Days 12–13 · Belize breathing space

Use these days for the coast, an island or a slower transition before continuing into Guatemala. The exact overnight base depends on the chosen route.

Day 14 · Cross into Guatemala

Use the official Melchor de Mencos crossing and continue to Flores or the Lake Petén Itzá area. Border procedures and transport are reconfirmed before departure.

Days 15–17 · Tikal

Explore Tikal National Park over one or more visits, with time for a guided interpretation of its monumental architecture, plazas and forest setting.

Days 18–20 · Petén & Yaxhá

Discover more of Petén at a slower pace, with an optional visit to Yaxhá, lake landscapes and community-led experiences where available.

Days 21–22 · Return through Belize

Travel back through the official border crossing and continue toward northern Belize. This return leg creates a practical connection for the Mexico extension.

Days 23–24 · Chetumal & Bacalar, Mexico

Cross into Mexico through the northern Belize–Chetumal corridor. Take time to settle in and discover the lagoon landscape around Bacalar.

Days 25–28 · Tulum & the Caribbean coast

Continue north to Tulum. Explore the coastal archaeological site, local landscapes and selected cenote or nature experiences with time to enjoy the region beyond a day trip.

Days 29–30 · More of Quintana Roo

Choose an additional Maya site such as Cobá or Muyil, or a nature-focused day, subject to current access, conservation rules and group interests.

Days 31–32 · Integration & free time

Keep the final days flexible for the sea, rest, local discoveries or an additional experience selected by the group.

Day 33 · Departure

Depart from the agreed airport or transfer point. The final overnight location will be chosen to match confirmed flight connections.
