Travel with BLINKITA

Follow the story of the Maya world.

These are guided, multi-day travel concepts connecting places, landscapes, cultural heritage and selected encounters across Belize and neighbouring Maya regions. They are distinct from BLINKITA wellness retreats: the focus here is exploration, discovery and the wider journey.

Each itinerary will be shaped around the route, season, group and practical travel arrangements. The places shown are proposed highlights, not a promise that every stop is included in every departure.

Three ways to explore

Choose your journey.

Begin with Belize, travel deeper into Guatemala, or take more time to explore selected parts of Mexico as well.

13 days · Belize

13 Days · Belize

A first, immersive introduction to Belize: Caribbean coast, rainforest, rivers, local communities and selected ancient Maya sites. A balanced journey with time to explore, enjoy nature and experience the region beyond the usual resort stay.

Possible highlights: Hopkins and the Caribbean coast · Cayo and Xunantunich · Lamanai or Altun Ha · rainforest and river experiences.

20 days · Belize & Guatemala

20 Days · Belize & Guatemala

A deeper exploration of the Maya world, combining Belize’s archaeological landscapes and living culture with a journey into Guatemala’s Petén region and the monumental city of Tikal.

Possible highlights: Belize’s Cayo region · official border crossing · Flores · Tikal National Park · jungle and lake landscapes.

33 days · Belize, Guatemala & Mexico

The Living Time Expedition

Our most extensive journey through the wider Maya region. Alongside Belize and Guatemala, the route may continue north into Mexico’s Yucatán Peninsula, with time for Tulum and other carefully selected places.

Possible highlights: Belize’s southern, central and northern regions · Tikal and Petén · Chetumal and Bacalar · Tulum and the Caribbean coast of Quintana Roo.
Enquire about 33 days ↗

Routes, border crossings, transport, accommodation, entry requirements and site access will be checked for each departure. Tulum is a substantial onward journey from the Belize–Mexico border area, not a brief side trip; the 33-day itinerary allows room to plan it properly.

The journey is the experience

Ancient places. Living cultures. A world to discover.

From jungle-covered archaeological reserves and river journeys in Belize to Tikal in Guatemala and the coastal Maya city of Tulum in Mexico, the wider region offers many different ways to encounter the depth and diversity of Maya history and contemporary life.

These journeys are designed with respect for the communities, traditions and heritage of the places we visit.
