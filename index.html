THE CARIBBEAN COAST · BELIZE

Where culture lives in the rhythm.

Hopkins is known for its vibrant Garifuna culture, where music, drumming, dance, food and oral traditions carry stories from one generation to the next.

In 2008, UNESCO recognized the language, dance and music of the Garifuna as Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

A connection to the place, its people and the rhythms of everyday life.

Here, the sea sets the rhythm. Nature opens space to breathe. Warm light, tropical colours and the easy pace of everyday life invite you to slow down, look around and experience Belize beyond the postcard.

Discover Belize

Things To Do

From snorkeling the coral reefs to exploring rainforest trails and vibrant Garifuna culture, Seiri Del Mar puts the best of Belize within easy reach. Every day brings a new adventure, or a chance to simply unwind by the sea.

A Friendsgiving Escape to the Tropics

Gather your favourite people for a tropical escape filled with ocean adventures, warm Caribbean breezes and time to reconnect. Explore Belize's vibrant marine life with guided snorkeling trips and diving adventures along the coastline.

Sea Tours

Discover the beauty of the Belize coastline on an ocean adventure. Enjoy the clear Caribbean waters, colourful marine life and unforgettable views.

Archaeological Tours

Explore the ancient Maya world and discover the history, architecture and cultural heritage that make Belize remarkable.

Zipline and Rappelling

Soar through the jungle canopy on a thrilling zipline adventure, then descend into the rainforest with a guided rappelling experience. Enjoy sweeping views, lush greenery and Belize's wild side.

Bike Tour

Slow down and explore Belize at your own pace. Ride through local villages and scenic roads, meet friendly locals and discover hidden corners.

Monkey River Tour

Journey along mangrove-lined waterways teeming with wildlife. Look out for howler monkeys, tropical birds and other jungle residents as your guide shares local stories and insights into this remarkable ecosystem.
