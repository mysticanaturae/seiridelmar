You can choose one session and let that be enough. You can continue step by step, explore a deeper programme or one day make Belize part of your story. The pace and the direction remain yours.

The method behind BLINKITA

A meeting place for ancient wisdom and the life you are creating.

BLINKITA is a holistic method created by Blanka Thomas, known as Maginja Blinkita. Its five pillars, BODY, MIND, TIME, LIFE and CIRCLE, bring together different paths of learning, wellbeing, personal transformation and connection.

It brings together years of study, practice and lived experience across different traditions and contemporary approaches. Each path has its own language and depth, yet they meet in one intention: to help people listen more closely to themselves, recognise what is asking for change and take a meaningful next step.
